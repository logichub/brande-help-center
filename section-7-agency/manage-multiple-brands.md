# Manage Multiple Client Brands

Each brand in your Agency Plan workspace is independent: separate Brand DNA, separate Content Agent, separate projects, separate team access.

Switching between brands is instantaneous. Your entire interface context changes—you see only that brand's data.

## Switch Brands

Click your profile icon in the top right corner.

Select **Brands** from the dropdown menu.

The Brands dropdown displays:
- A list of all brands you own or have access to
- A crown icon next to each brand you own
- A **+** button to create a new brand

Click any brand name to switch to it immediately.

The interface updates:
- Dashboard shows that brand's projects and calendar
- Account sidebar shows that brand's Brand Profile, Brand Voice, Brand Materials
- Content Agent recommendations are generated for that brand only
- Team members only see brands they're invited to

No need to log out or re-authenticate. One click, and you're working in a completely different brand context.

## Brand Details: What's Separate

When you switch brands, these elements are entirely independent:

**Brand Profile (Brand DNA)**
- Business objectives
- Target audience and triggers
- Content channels
- Brand voice and assets
- Custom fields (if configured)

**Content Agent**
Each brand has its own Content Agent that generates personalized recommendations based on that brand's specific DNA.

Client A's Content Agent might recommend: "Create a LinkedIn post about hiring challenges in early-stage startups" based on Client A's audience profile.

Client B's Content Agent might recommend: "Write an email about integrating with Zapier" based on Client B's product positioning.

**Projects and Folders**
Each brand maintains its own project library, folders, and content calendar. Switching brands changes what you see in the Projects sidebar.

**Brand Voice Profiles**
If you've created multiple voice profiles for one brand, they're tied to that specific brand and won't appear when you switch to another brand.

**Brand Assets**
Reference materials, brand guidelines, snippets, and custom content blocks are stored per-brand.

**Team Access**
Team members are invited to specific brands. A team member invited to Client A brand does not see Client B brand—it doesn't appear in their Brands dropdown at all.

## Ownership and Permissions

Look for the crown icon next to your brand name in the Brands dropdown.

**Crown Icon = Brand Owner**
You can edit Brand Profile, create projects, manage collaborators, and delete content for that brand.

**No Crown Icon = Brand Collaborator**
You have either:
- Full editing access (if invited as "Agency Admin")
- Read-only + approval access (if invited as "Client Collaborator")

Brand owners can change a collaborator's role or remove them at any time.

## Brand-Level Settings

When you switch to a brand, all Account sidebar options apply to *that brand only*:

- **Brand Profile** — Edit that brand's DNA
- **Brand Voice** — Create or edit voice profiles for that brand
- **Content Opportunities** — See that brand's Content Agent recommendations
- **Brand Messaging Framework** — Configure messaging for that brand
- **Brand Materials** — Store reference materials for that brand
- **Content Reference** — Add examples and assets for that brand
- **Snippets** — Create reusable text blocks for that brand
- **Custom Fields** — Configure project metadata for that brand

Global workspace settings that apply across all brands:
- **My Plan** — Your subscription level (shared across all brands)
- **API Keys** — Workspace-level API keys (not brand-specific)
- **Checklist Templates** — Reusable across all brands

## Content Calendar Across Brands

The Content Calendar shows projects for the currently selected brand.

To view one brand's calendar:
1. Click profile icon > select brand
2. Go to dashboard or open Content Calendar from sidebar
3. You see only that brand's pending projects and scheduled items

To compare calendars across clients:
- Switch brands and take note of project deadlines
- Or manage all brands' calendars manually in external tools

## Best Practices for Multi-Brand Management

**Organize Your Sidebar**
Pin favorite brands to the top of the Brands dropdown for quick switching. Use a consistent naming convention: "Client: Brand Name" or "2024-ClientName" for easy scanning.

**Use Checklists Across Brands**
Create checklist templates in your workspace that apply to every brand. Assign the same approval checklist to every project, every client.

**Consistency Without Duplication**
Use Snippets (reusable text blocks) to maintain consistent messaging across brands. Create a snippet for your CTA, your company name, your value prop. Reuse it across all clients.

**Team Assignment by Brand**
Invite team members to 2-4 brands only—enough to stay busy, focused enough to build expertise in those clients. Avoid spreading team thin across 10+ brands.

**Separate Admin vs. Creator Access**
Invite strategists with full Brand Profile editing access. Invite creators with project-creation-only access. This prevents accidental Brand DNA changes.

## Troubleshooting

**Q: A team member can't see a brand I created.**
A: You must explicitly invite them to that brand. Creating a brand doesn't automatically give access to all team members. Use the Invite modal to add them.

**Q: I switched brands but the sidebar still shows the old brand's data.**
A: Refresh your browser. Sometimes the sidebar cache doesn't update immediately after switching.
