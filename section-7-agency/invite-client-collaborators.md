# Invite Client Collaborators

Client collaborators need to review and approve your content before publication. Grant them read-only access to projects, approval rights, and nothing else.

Brande.ai handles the invitation, role assignment, and access control automatically.

## Step 1: Open the Invite Modal

**Option A: From Account Sidebar**
1. Click your profile icon (top right) or press **Ctrl/Alt+A**
2. Open Account sidebar
3. Scroll to find **Invite** button or modal link
4. Click **Invite**

**Option B: From Profile Dropdown**
1. Click your profile icon (top right)
2. Select **Invite** from the dropdown menu

The Invite modal opens immediately.

## Step 2: Enter Collaborator Email

In the **Email address** field, type the client collaborator's email.

The field shows placeholder text: "Ex: johndoe@gmail.com"

Validation rules:
- Email field is required
- Email must be in valid format (e.g., name@example.com)
- You cannot invite yourself
- A user can only be invited once per brand (no duplicate invites)

## Step 3: Select the Brand

In the **Select Brand** dropdown, choose which client brand to invite them to.

Dropdown displays:
- All brands you own or manage
- Brand names with clear labels

You can only invite them to brands you have access to.

The invite dialog accepts one brand per invitation — to add the same person to multiple brands, send a separate invite for each.

## Step 4: Select the Role

Choose the collaborator's role:

Brande.ai exposes exactly two roles in the invite dialog:

**Brand Collaborator** (full editing access)
- Can view, create, and edit projects for the brand
- Can edit Brand Profile, Brand Voice, Reference Materials, and other brand settings
- For internal team members and agency staff

**Client Collaborator** (read-only with approval rights)
- Can view all projects and content for this brand
- Can approve or unapprove projects before publication
- Cannot edit Brand Profile, create projects, or delete content
- For clients, stakeholders, and reviewers

Select **Client Collaborator** for external clients and stakeholders, **Brand Collaborator** for everyone who needs to edit.

## Step 5: Send Invite

Click the **Send Invite** button.

Brande.ai sends an email invitation to the collaborator's email address. The email contains:
- A welcome message
- A link to join the brand
- Instructions on how to log in or create an account

Success message appears in the modal: "{email} has been invited to brand {brandName}"

The invite is now sent. You can close the modal.

## What the Collaborator Sees

When they log in (or create an account after following the invite link), they see:

**Brands Dropdown**
Only the brand(s) they've been invited to. If invited to Client A brand, they only see Client A in the Brands dropdown.

**Dashboard**
Projects and calendar for that brand only. No access to other client brands.

**Projects**
A full list of projects for their brand with two key additions:
- **Approve button** — Mark the project as approved
- **Unapprove button** — Reject approval if revisions are needed

**Approval Status**
Each project shows approval history:
- "Pending Approval"
- "Approved by [Collaborator Name] on [Date]"
- "Unapproved by [Collaborator Name] on [Date]" (if they reject)

**Read-Only Restrictions**
They cannot:
- Edit Brand Profile
- Create new projects
- Delete projects or content
- Modify brand voice or messaging
- Manage team members
- Access Account settings

They see a "Premium Feature" gate or disabled button if they try to access restricted areas.

## Managing Collaborator Access

After sending an invite:

**Resend an Invite**
There isn't a one-click resend in the current dialog. If the collaborator didn't receive the original email, send a new invite to the same address.

**Remove Access**
Remove the user from the brand using your standard team-management workflow. Once they're no longer a member of the brand, links and access stop working.

**Change Role**
There is no in-app role-change action today. To switch a user from Brand Collaborator to Client Collaborator (or vice versa), remove and re-invite them with the new role.

## Bulk Invites

Bulk invites are not supported in the dialog — send invites one at a time. For five client stakeholders on the same brand, that means five separate invites.

## Approval Workflow Best Practices

**Invite the Right People**
- Clients: Brand owner or CMO (decision maker)
- Internal review: Content strategist or quality lead
- Stakeholder feedback: Product, sales, or marketing manager

**Set Clear Expectations**
Mention in your kickoff email that client collaborators will receive approval requests for all projects, and their timely feedback keeps the content schedule on track.

**Use Project Names for Clarity**
Name projects clearly so approvers know what they're reviewing:
- "LinkedIn Post — Hiring Challenges (Approved)" instead of "Project_Draft_1"
- "Email — Product Launch Campaign (Pending Review)" instead of "Email_v2"

**Track Approval History**
Each project shows who approved it and when. This creates an audit trail for quality and accountability.

## Troubleshooting

**Q: The collaborator says they didn't receive the invite email.**
A: Check your email address for typos. Resend the invite. Ask them to check spam/junk folders. The invite email comes from Brande.ai with a verification link.

**Q: Can I invite someone who doesn't have a Brande.ai account yet?**
A: Yes. They receive an email invite with a link to create an account or log in. They don't need to pre-create an account.

**Q: What if they approve a project but I want to unapprove it?**
A: Any user with access to the project can click **Unapprove** to clear the approval. The project returns to draft and can be revised.

**Q: Can I invite the same person to multiple brands?**
A: Yes — send a separate invite per brand. There is no multi-brand invite shortcut.
