# Edit, Refine, and Regenerate Content

Generated content is never final. Brand DNA ensures the output is aligned and on-voice—but refinement is your control panel. The chat-based editor lets you request modifications, regenerate sections, and iterate without leaving the project. Up to 20 revision snapshots track your path from draft to published.

## The Editor Interface

When you open a project, you see:

**Left panel: Document Editor**
- Block-based editor (EditorJS)
- Click any block to edit text directly
- Drag blocks to rearrange sections
- Delete unwanted sections
- Add new blocks (headings, text, images, tables)

**Right panel: Chat Interface**
- Conversation history with the AI
- Request modifications to any section
- Ask for new sections or complete rewrites
- Watch regeneration happen in real-time

## Direct Text Editing

The fastest way to modify is direct editing:

1. **Click any text block** in the document
2. **Edit the text directly** — Rewrite headlines, adjust wording, add details
3. **Changes save automatically** — No "save" button needed

Use direct editing for:
- Typo fixes
- Small wording adjustments
- Adding a specific name or detail
- Removing a sentence or paragraph

## Chat-Based Refinement

For larger changes, request modification in chat:

**Chat Request Examples:**

"Make the headline more benefit-focused"
→ AI regenerates the headline to emphasize reader benefit instead of feature

"Add a customer quote in the second section"
→ AI inserts a realistic customer testimonial that supports your point

"Tone down the marketing language—make it more conversational"
→ AI rewrites the entire document to feel less corporate, more authentic

"Expand the CTA section with three specific next steps"
→ AI elaborates the call-to-action with concrete, actionable steps

"Shorten this to 500 words instead of 1000"
→ AI condenses while keeping the core message intact

"Add a section explaining the ROI of this approach"
→ AI inserts a new section with specific benefits and value proposition

"Rewrite the opening to address the objection: 'This sounds complicated'"
→ AI crafts an opening that directly addresses this concern and builds confidence

"Make this more vulnerable—include a mistake I made"
→ AI rewrites to include a personal learning moment or failure story

"Break this into 5 bullet points instead of paragraphs"
→ AI converts narrative into scannable bullet list format

"Add data or statistics to back up this claim"
→ AI incorporates relevant data points that support your main argument

## How Regeneration Works

When you request a modification in chat:

1. **AI reads your request** — Understands what you want changed
2. **AI references your Brand DNA** — Ensures changes match your voice, audience, and objectives
3. **AI regenerates the section** — Sends updated text back
4. **Updated section appears in editor** — You see the revision immediately
5. **Old version is saved** — You can revert if needed

**Regeneration respects existing content:**
- If you ask to "rewrite the first paragraph," the rest of the document stays the same
- If you ask to "add a new section after point 2," existing sections don't change
- If you ask for a full rewrite, the entire document gets regenerated while keeping the core structure

## Revision History

Brande.ai saves up to **20 revision snapshots** of your project. Access them to:
- Track what changed between versions
- Revert to an earlier version if a change didn't work

Revision history lives inline at the top of the editor. When the project has more than one revision, a **‹** / **›** chevron pair plus a **Revision X of N** label appears above the editor. Step through revisions with the chevrons; when viewing any non-current revision, a pencil icon labelled **Make this the current version** appears so you can promote it. There is no separate revisions sidebar or menu — everything is in this inline strip.

**Typical revision snapshots:**
1. First generation (after clicking Done)
2. First refinement (after your first chat request)
3. Second refinement (after your second chat request)
...and so on, up to 20 total

## Specific Refinement Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Website Copy Needs Stronger CTA

**Generated CTA:**
"Click below to learn more about our pricing."

**Your request in chat:**
"Make the CTA more specific and urgent. I want to move them to a discovery call, not just learn more."

**Result:**
"Ready to understand how we can transform your revenue operations? Schedule a 20-minute strategy call with our fractional CFO—no prep needed."

### Scenario 2: LinkedIn Post Feels Too Corporate

**Generated post:**
"We are pleased to announce our new partnership with [Client]. This strategic alliance positions us for continued market leadership..."

**Your request in chat:**
"Tone down the corporate language. Make this conversational and authentic. This should feel like it's from a real person."

**Result:**
"One of my favorite partnerships just went public. Here's what made this work: we found a team that values relationships over transactions. Excited to see what we build together."

### Scenario 3: Blog Post Missing Personal Story

**Generated intro:**
"Hiring for fit is important. Here are the top reasons why..."

**Your request in chat:**
"Add a personal mistake story. I want to start with something vulnerable about how I learned this the hard way."

**Result:**
"I hired the most experienced engineer on the market. Six months later, she left because the culture wasn't her fit. That mistake taught me something I wish I'd learned sooner: experience is just credentials; fit is everything."

### Scenario 4: Content Too Long

**Generated article:**
1,800 words

**Your request in chat:**
"This is too long for a blog post. Trim this to 800 words by focusing only on the three main points. Remove examples and sidebars."

**Result:**
AI condenses to 800 words, keeps the three main points, removes supporting examples and tangents.

### Scenario 5: Missing Specific Data

**Generated claim:**
"This approach saves time."

**Your request in chat:**
"I need to back this up with data. We found it saved our clients 15 hours per month. Can you add that?"

**Result:**
"This approach saves time—specifically, our clients average 15 hours per month recovered. That's equivalent to a full-time contractor's capacity."

## Best Practices for Effective Refinement

**Best Practice 1: Be specific in your request**
Vague: "Make this better"
Specific: "Make this more benefit-focused by starting with what the reader gains, not what we do"

**Best Practice 2: Reference the section you want changed**
Instead of: "Rewrite the third point"
Say: "Rewrite the section about ROI calculations"

**Best Practice 3: Explain the "why" behind your request**
Instead of: "Make this shorter"
Say: "Make this shorter because we're targeting busy founders who scan, not read"

**Best Practice 4: Request one major change at a time**
Instead of: "Rewrite the entire document, change the tone, add a personal story, and make it shorter"
Say: "Rewrite the opening with a personal story first" (then request other changes after)

**Best Practice 5: Use revision history to test ideas**
Generate a version, request a risky change, see if you like it. If not, revert to a previous snapshot and try a different direction.

**Best Practice 6: Lock in what's working**
If the first 3 sections are perfect, request changes only to sections 4–6. Don't regenerate the whole document.

## Status Workflow During Refinement

As you refine, update your project status to track progress:

- **Planning** — Initial generation, exploratory phase
- **Draft** — Content is roughly right, in refinement phase
- **Review** — Content is ready for feedback from team or client
- **Approved** — Stakeholder signed off, ready to publish
- **Published** — Live on your channel

Use status changes to signal where your project is in the workflow.

## Collaborative Refinement

If you're working with a team:

1. **Generate content** in Brande.ai
2. **Set status to Review** to signal it's ready for feedback
3. **Share project** with your team or client
4. **Collect feedback** via comments or email
5. **Implement feedback** using chat refinement (one request per feedback item)
6. **Set status to Approved** once stakeholder signs off
7. **Publish** when ready

Each refinement saves a new revision snapshot, so your team can see the evolution of the content.

## Tips for Efficient Refinement

**Tip 1: Generate first, refine second**
Get the first version out quickly. Don't overthink variables before generation. The AI will refactor on request.

**Tip 2: Trust your instinct**
If something feels off in the voice, tone, or message, request a change. Refinement is cheap; shipping misaligned content is expensive.

**Tip 3: Use direct editing for tiny fixes**
Typos, word swaps, minor edits go faster with direct clicking and editing than chat requests.

**Tip 4: Use chat for structural changes**
Rewriting sections, adding content, changing tone—these are chat requests. The AI handles larger transformations better in conversation.

**Tip 5: Save your best refinement requests**
If you find a request that consistently improves content (e.g., "Make this more vulnerable and specific"), use it on every project.

**Tip 6: One round of external feedback**
Avoid endless revisions. Get one round of feedback from your team or client, implement it, and move forward. Perfection is the enemy of shipping.

## Troubleshooting Refinement

**"The AI didn't make the change I requested"**
→ Restate the request with more specific direction. Example: "In the second paragraph, change 'we help businesses' to 'we help founders who are...'

**"It keeps reverting to corporate language"**
→ The Brand DNA may be pulling corporate tone. Request: "Use the voice of [specific founder/person], not corporate marketing."

**"I want to go back to an earlier version"**
→ Open the project, use the **‹** chevron above the editor to step back to the revision you want, then click the **Make this the current version** pencil icon to promote it.

**"Changes to one section are affecting another section"**
→ Request each section change separately. Request full document rewrites only when you want comprehensive changes.
