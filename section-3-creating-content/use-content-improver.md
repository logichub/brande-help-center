# Use the Content Improver

Generated content is functional. Improved content converts. **Content Improver** is one of Brande.ai's project templates (under the Copywriting category). You paste an existing piece of content into the variable dialog, pick the improvement modes you want applied, and Brande.ai rewrites the content using your Brand DNA.

![Use the Content Improver — Brande.ai](./images/use-content-improver.jpg)


Use it when you have a draft that's close but not landing — a launch announcement that feels flat, a LinkedIn post that's too corporate, a landing-page section that buries the value.

## What Content Improver Does

The Content Improver runs analysis on your generated or edited content and identifies:

**Brand Alignment Issues:**
- Voice mismatches (too corporate when it should be conversational)
- Messaging that doesn't reflect Brand DNA
- Audience language mismatch (too broad instead of specific)

**Conversion Issues:**
- Weak or missing CTAs
- Unclear value proposition
- Objection handling gaps
- Social proof or credibility gaps

**Structure and Flow Issues:**
- Weak headlines that don't hook
- Unclear narrative arc
- Sections in wrong order
- Information density (too dense or too sparse)

**Tone and Engagement Issues:**
- Passive voice when active would punch harder
- Generic language when specific would resonate
- Missing personal story or vulnerability
- Overly formal when conversational fits better

## Where Content Improver Lives

Content Improver is a project template, not a separate panel inside the editor. You reach it through the new-project flow:

1. From the **Projects** page or dashboard, start a new project
2. In the **Create a new project** dialog, search for or scroll to **Content Improver** (Copywriting category)
3. Select it to open the template's variable dialog

## How to Use Content Improver

### Step 1: Have your draft ready

Copy the content you want to improve. It can be content Brande.ai generated earlier or content from outside the app.

### Step 2: Open the Content Improver template

From the Create a new project dialog, choose **Content Improver**. The variable dialog opens.

### Step 3: Review Improvement Suggestions

Content Improver analyzes your draft and displays:

**A score or health indicator** (e.g., "Content Alignment: 72%")

**Category breakdown:**
- Brand Voice Alignment
- Audience Connection
- Conversion Readiness
- Structure & Clarity

**Specific suggestions** for each area:

*Example:*
- "Your opening headline addresses pain but doesn't create urgency. Try: 'This hiring mistake costs you $250k+ per year.'"
- "CTA is generic ('Learn more'). Your audience responds better to specific outcomes. Try: 'Get your 90-day hiring playbook.'"
- "Second paragraph feels corporate. You usually add personal stories early. Consider: 'I made this mistake 3 times before I figured it out.'"

### Step 4: Implement Suggestions in Chat

Read through suggestions. Choose ones that resonate.

**In the chat panel, request:**
"Apply the headline suggestion from Content Improver"
→ AI regenerates the headline with the specific suggestion

Or ask a follow-up question:
"I like the CTA suggestion but want it more adventurous. Try 'Unlock your competitive advantage' instead."
→ AI adapts

Not all suggestions will be right for your piece. Skip ones that don't fit your voice or goal.

### Step 5: Re-run Content Improver

After implementing improvements, re-run Content Improver to see updated scores.

**Expected flow:**
1. First generation → Run Content Improver → Score: 65%
2. Implement 3 suggestions → Run Content Improver → Score: 82%
3. Implement 2 more → Run Content Improver → Score: 89%

You're aiming for alignment, not perfection. 80%+ is strong.

## Improvement Modes

Content Improver doesn't score your content — it rewrites it. You pick one or more improvement modes from the variable dialog, and the rewrite emphasises those goals while keeping your Brand DNA intact:

- Strengthen writing clarity and flow
- Make it more concise
- Boost brand voice and alignment
- Humanize the content
- Increase persuasiveness

**Pro tip:** if you originally generated the source content in Brande.ai, attach that earlier project as a Reference Project in the variable dialog. The improver then has full context for the rewrite.

**Brand Alignment (0–100%)**
How well does this content match your Brand DNA, voice, and audience? Higher is better.
- 80%+ = Content feels authentically yours
- 60–79% = Content is on-brand but could be more specific
- Below 60% = Content feels generic or misaligned

**Audience Connection (0–100%)**
Does this content speak directly to your audience's pain, language, and triggers?
- 80%+ = Strong audience resonance
- 60–79% = Adequate but could be more specific
- Below 60% = Generic; could alienate instead of connect

**Conversion Readiness (0–100%)**
Is the content structured to move readers toward your desired action?
- 80%+ = Clear path to action; strong CTA
- 60–79% = CTA exists but could be clearer or more compelling
- Below 60% = Missing CTA or CTA feels buried

**Structure & Clarity (0–100%)**
Is the content easy to follow? Do ideas flow logically?
- 80%+ = Clear narrative arc; easy to follow
- 60–79% = Readable but could be more scannable or organized
- Below 60% = Confusing; reader gets lost

## Content Improver Suggestions: Examples

### Example 1: Website Copy

**Suggestion:** "Your value proposition states features, not benefits. Instead of 'Built-in analytics dashboard,' try 'See exactly where your revenue leaks—and plug them.'"

**Implementation:** In chat: "Rewrite the value prop to focus on benefit, not feature. Here's the suggested angle: 'See exactly where your revenue leaks—and plug them.'"

### Example 2: LinkedIn Post

**Suggestion:** "Your hook is too broad. You usually open with a specific, surprising statement. Try: 'We fired our VP of Sales last month. Best decision we made.'"

**Implementation:** In chat: "Apply the hook suggestion: 'We fired our VP of Sales last month. Best decision we made.' Then explain why."

### Example 3: Blog Post

**Suggestion:** "The article is 2,000 words but the core argument could land in 1,200. Consider cutting the second case study to maintain reader attention."

**Implementation:** In chat: "Trim this to 1,200 words by removing the second case study example. Keep the methodology and first customer story."

### Example 4: Email Campaign

**Suggestion:** "CTA reads 'Click here to learn more.' Your audience responds to specific outcomes. Try: 'Get your personalized 30-day growth plan.'"

**Implementation:** In chat: "Change the CTA to 'Get your personalized 30-day growth plan' and make the button text match."

## Best Practices for Content Improver

**Best Practice 1: Run Content Improver after each major revision**
Don't wait until you think you're done. After chat refinements, re-run Content Improver to see if you've improved alignment.

**Best Practice 2: Prioritize Audience Connection over other scores**
If you have to choose between improving Audience Connection or Conversion Readiness, choose Audience Connection first. A connected audience is more likely to convert.

**Best Practice 3: Don't chase 100%**
80–85% is the sweet spot. Some suggestions might be off, or not fit your specific goals. Aim for strong alignment, not perfection.

**Best Practice 4: Trust your instinct over the tool**
If Content Improver suggests something that contradicts your Brand DNA, skip it. You know your brand better than any tool.

**Best Practice 5: Track which suggestions you implement**
After publishing, note which improvements drove engagement. Pattern match on what works with your audience.

**Best Practice 6: Use Content Improver as a learning tool**
Over time, you'll see patterns in suggestions. "My CTAs are always too generic," or "My openings lack urgency." Use these patterns to improve your default writing.

## Troubleshooting Content Improver

**"Content Improver score dropped after my refinement"**
→ You may have made a change that drifted from Brand DNA. Look at the new suggestions. They'll clarify what shifted.

**"Some suggestions feel off or not right for my brand"**
→ Content Improver is a guide, not gospel. Skip suggestions that don't fit. The tool is learning, and so are you.

**"The rewrite kept too much / too little of my original"**
→ The improvement modes you selected drive how aggressive the rewrite is. Re-run with different modes selected, or refine in the editor's chat afterward.

**"I want to skip one improvement mode"**
→ Just don't tick it in the variable dialog when starting the project. Each mode is independent.

## Content Improver Workflow

1. **Generate content** → Full draft appears
2. **Make initial edits** → Direct edits in editor + chat refinements
3. **Run Content Improver** → Scores and suggestions appear
4. **Implement priority suggestions** → Chat requests for changes
5. **Re-run Content Improver** → Scores improve (hopefully)
6. **Repeat steps 4–5** until aligned
7. **Publish** when score is 80%+

## Content Improver Limitations

**Content Improver does not:**
- Write content for you (you must implement suggestions via chat)
- Guarantee higher engagement (suggestions are strong practices, not guarantees)
- Replace human judgment (your instinct matters more)
- Handle niche or specialized audiences well (works best for B2B SaaS, less nuanced for entertainment or lifestyle)

**Content Improver works best for:**
- Website copy and landing pages
- LinkedIn posts
- Blog post introductions
- Email subject lines and CTAs
- Any brand-facing content
