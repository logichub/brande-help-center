# Use Reference Materials to Ground Your Content

Reference materials are real data, statistics, case studies, and examples that anchor your content in truth.

When you add reference materials, Brande.ai weaves them into content generation—replacing vague claims with specific evidence.

## What Reference Materials Are

Reference materials are fact-based inputs that make your content more credible and specific.

Examples:

- **Statistics:** "45% of founders cite cash flow as their top pain point"
- **Case studies:** "ABC Corp reduced onboarding time by 60% using our platform"
- **Customer data:** "Our customers are 70% based in the US, 30% in Europe"
- **Product details:** "Our tool integrates with Stripe, HubSpot, and Zapier"
- **Market data:** "The fractional CFO market is growing 20% YoY"
- **Testimonials:** "Our product helped us scale from 10 to 50 customers in 6 months"

When Brande.ai creates content about your offering, it references these materials instead of generating generic claims.

## Where to Add Reference Materials

Navigate to **Account** → **Content Reference**.

You'll see a list of existing materials and an add button.

Click **Add Reference** or **+** to create a new material.

## Content Types for Reference Materials

### Text

Write or paste direct quotes, statistics, facts, or brief descriptions.

**Example:**
```
45% of marketing teams report content planning as their biggest time bottleneck, according to HubSpot's 2024 State of Marketing report
```

**Good for:** Statistics, quotes, specific product features, customer insights

### File

Upload a document containing data, case studies, research, or reference information.

Supported: PDF, Word, text files

**Example:** Customer success story PDF, market research document, competitor analysis

**Good for:** Longer reference material, case studies, market research, product specifications

### Media File

Upload video, audio, or image files that provide reference context.

Supported: MP4, MOV, MP3, WAV, PNG, JPG

**Example:** Customer testimonial video, product demo video, screenshot of results

**Good for:** Customer testimonials, product demonstrations, before/after visuals

### URL

Link to external sources of truth without uploading files.

**Example:** "https://example.com/case-study-acme" or "https://linkedin.com/company/brande-ai"

**Good for:** Published articles, case studies, external research, company pages

## Types of Reference Materials to Add

### Customer & Success Data

**Examples:**
- "Client ABC reduced content creation time from 8 hours to 2 hours per week"
- "78% of our clients see increased engagement within 30 days"
- "Average ROI for our clients: 3.2x within 6 months"

**Why it matters:** Proof points convince prospects. Brande.ai will reference this in sales content, blog posts, and paid ads.

### Product Features & Integrations

**Examples:**
- "Integrations: Stripe, HubSpot, Zapier, Slack, Airtable, Google Drive"
- "Supports 28 AI models including OpenAI, Anthropic, Perplexity"
- "Real-time collaboration with up to 10 team members per project"

**Why it matters:** When writing product content, Brande.ai grounds copy in actual capabilities, not guesses.

### Market Data & Industry Trends

**Examples:**
- "B2B SaaS content budgets grew 35% YoY (Gartner 2024)"
- "LinkedIn has 900 million professional users"
- "Video content gets 80% higher engagement than text"

**Why it matters:** When creating thought leadership content, Brande.ai references real market data instead of generic trends.

### Competitive Positioning

**Examples:**
- "Unlike traditional marketing agencies, we deliver content in hours, not weeks"
- "Our Brand DNA approach is 3x faster than prompt-based tools"
- "We're the only platform that analyzes competitor content automatically"

**Why it matters:** When writing brand messaging, Brande.ai can differentiate your offering with specificity.

### Testimonials & Social Proof

**Examples:**
- Quote from customer: "This tool changed how we think about content strategy"
- LinkedIn review: "Best brand positioning tool we've used"
- Case study excerpt: "Within 3 months, our content quality improved by 40%"

**Why it matters:** Brande.ai will reference these in promotional content and case studies.

### Educational or Methodology Content

**Examples:**
- Link to your blog article: "The StoryBrand Framework for SaaS"
- Link to your YouTube video: "How to Identify Your Ideal Customer"
- Whitepaper excerpt: "The ROI Model for Content Marketing"

**Why it matters:** When creating educational content, Brande.ai can reference and expand on your existing methodology.

## How to Add Reference Materials Effectively

### Be specific, not generic

**Weak:**
> "Our customers love our product and see great results"

**Strong:**
> "78% of our customers report increased engagement within 30 days. Average content creation time dropped from 5 hours to 2 hours per project."

### Include the source

If citing statistics or research, note the source.

**Good format:**
> "45% of marketing teams report content planning as their biggest bottleneck (HubSpot State of Marketing 2024)"

### Keep materials current

Update reference materials quarterly.

If a case study is outdated or a statistic is older than a year, refresh it.

Brande.ai will incorporate current data, not legacy claims.

### Organize by category

Label materials clearly so you (and Brande.ai) know what they're for.

**Example labels:**
- "Customer Success: Time Saved"
- "Product Feature: AI Integration"
- "Market Trend: Content Demand"
- "Testimonial: CEO Quote"
- "Competitive Positioning: Speed vs Agencies"

### Add 5–10 materials minimum

One reference material is almost useless.

Aim for 5–10 across different categories (customer data, product details, market trends, testimonials).

This gives Brande.ai enough truth to work with.

## How Reference Materials Affect Content

When you create content, Brande.ai references these materials in its output.

**Example: You create a LinkedIn post using a Content Agent recommendation**

Without reference materials:
> "Join thousands of marketers who are scaling their content production. Our platform helps teams work faster and smarter."

With reference materials:
> "78% of marketing teams report content planning as their biggest bottleneck. We help teams reduce content creation time from 5 hours to 2 hours per project. See how ABC Corp achieved this in 6 months."

Specificity drives engagement and conversion.

The more quality reference materials you provide, the more powerful your content becomes.
