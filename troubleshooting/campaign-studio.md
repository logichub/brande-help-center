# Campaign Studio Not Working

## Research Sprint Seems Stuck

**Direct Answer:** A research sprint that hasn't made progress for a few minutes is flagged as stalled, and Brande.ai offers a Resume option instead of leaving it spinning indefinitely.

![Campaign Studio Not Working — Brande.ai](../section-11-campaign-studio/images/understanding-campaign-studio.jpg)


### How to Fix This

1. **Wait a few minutes**
   - Research sprints involve multiple steps running in sequence; some steps take longer than others
   - The activity feed updates automatically as each step completes

2. **Look for the Resume button**
   - If there's been no progress for a few minutes, or the run failed, a **Resume** action appears
   - Click it — this picks up from whatever research is already complete rather than starting over

3. **Confirm it didn't cost you a run**
   - Resuming a stalled or failed sprint does not use up another monthly campaign run
   - Only starting a brand-new research sprint counts against your quota

### If This Doesn't Work

- **Refresh the page** — clears any stale client-side state
- **Contact support** — include the campaign name and roughly when it stalled

## Can't Generate the Blueprint

**Direct Answer:** Blueprint generation requires all five research sections to be approved first.

### How to Fix This

1. **Go back to the Review stage**
   - Check each of the five research sections: Audience Insights, Competitive Landscape, Cultural & Search Trends, Voice Alignment, Recommended Angles
   - Any section without the approve toggle switched on will block blueprint generation

2. **Approve the remaining sections**
   - If you disagree with a section, leave a note explaining what you'd like changed before approving — your note is used when the blueprint is generated
   - Once every section is approved, the Generate Blueprint action becomes available

### If This Doesn't Work

- **Contact support** if a section appears approved but blueprint generation still won't start

## Hit the Monthly Campaign Limit

**Direct Answer:** Your plan's monthly research-run quota is used up for this calendar month.

### How to Fix This

1. **Check your usage**
   - Go to **Account > My Plan** to see your current plan and usage
   - The quota is counted from when research runs are started, reset each calendar month

2. **Remember the quota is shared per brand**
   - The limit is counted against the brand owner's plan, not per collaborator
   - If your brand has multiple collaborators starting campaigns, they're all drawing from the same monthly allowance

3. **Upgrade or wait**
   - Click **Upgrade Now** on the limit banner to move to a plan with a higher monthly quota
   - Otherwise, wait for next month's quota to reset

### If This Doesn't Work

- **Contact support** if your usage count looks wrong

---

## Campaign Studio Isn't Available in My Account

**Direct Answer:** Your current plan doesn't include Campaign Studio.

### How to Fix This

1. **Check your plan**
   - Go to **Account > My Plan**
   - Compare your current plan's included features against what Campaign Studio requires

2. **Upgrade your plan**
   - Click **Upgrade Now** on the access banner shown in place of the Campaign Studio dashboard
   - Upgrades take effect immediately

### If This Doesn't Work

- **Contact support** if you believe Campaign Studio should already be included in your plan
