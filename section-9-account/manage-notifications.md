# Manage Notifications

Notifications keep you informed about project approvals, team activity, and important updates without being intrusive.

Control what you're notified about and how you're notified from your Notifications settings.

## Access Notifications

**Option A: Notifications Icon (Quick Access)**
In the header (top of the page), click the **Notifications icon** (bell icon or similar).

The notifications panel slides open showing your recent notifications.

**Option B: Profile Dropdown**
1. Click your profile icon (top right)
2. Select **Notifications** from the dropdown
3. Notifications page loads

**Option C: Sidebar Navigation**

## Notifications Page

The Notifications page displays:

**Notification List**
- Unread notifications at the top
- Read notifications below
- Each notification shows:
  - What happened (e.g., "Project approved by Sarah")
  - Who it involves
  - When it happened
  - An action link (e.g., "View Project")

**Pagination**
If you have many notifications, they're displayed in pages (e.g., 20 per page).

**Mark as Read/Unread**
Click a notification to mark it as read. Or click a checkbox to select multiple and mark as read in bulk.

**Delete Notifications**
[NEEDS REVIEW: whether you can delete individual notifications]

Select notifications and click Delete to remove them.

## Notification Types

You may receive notifications for:

**Project Approvals**
"Sarah Martinez approved your LinkedIn Post — Hiring Content project"
- Shows who approved
- Shows which project
- Action: Click to view the project

**Project Rejections**
"Sarah Martinez unapproved your LinkedIn Post — Hiring Content project"
- Shows who unapproved
- Shows why (if they left a comment)
- Action: Click to view and revise the project

**Team Member Invites**
"You've been invited to the [Brand Name] brand"
- Shows who invited you
- Shows which brand
- Action: Click to accept or view invite

**Team Activity** (if enabled)
"John created a new project: Blog — Product Updates"
- Shows team member action
- Shows what they did
- Action: Click to view the project

**Content Agent Recommendations**
"New Content Agent recommendations are available"
- Shows how many recommendations
- Action: Click to view recommendations

**Plan Reminders**
"You're approaching your project limit for this month"
- Shows which limit you're approaching
- Action: Click to upgrade plan

**System Updates**
"Brande.ai has a new feature: Enhanced image generation"
- Shows what's new
- Action: Click to learn more

## Notification Settings

[NEEDS REVIEW: exact notification preferences UI]

Click the **Settings** button or link in the Notifications page or profile dropdown to access notification preferences.

**Possible Settings:**

### Notification Types
Toggle which notification types you want to receive:
- [ ] Project approvals
- [ ] Project rejections
- [ ] Team member activity
- [ ] Content Agent updates
- [ ] Plan reminders
- [ ] System updates

**Uncheck to disable notifications** for that type.

### Notification Frequency
Choose how often you receive notifications:
- **Real-Time** — Instant notification as things happen
- **Digest** — One email per day summarizing activity
- **Weekly** — One email per week summarizing activity
- **Never** — Disable notifications

### Notification Channels
Choose how you're notified:
- [ ] In-App notifications (Notifications icon in header)
- [ ] Email notifications
- [ ] Push notifications (if you've enabled browser notifications)

**Uncheck to disable** that notification channel.

### Do Not Disturb Hours
[NEEDS REVIEW: whether DND settings exist]

Set a time range (e.g., 6 PM - 9 AM) when notifications are suppressed. Urgent notifications may still come through.

## Notification Badge Count

The Notifications icon in the header displays a badge with the count of unread notifications.

For example: A bell icon with a red "3" badge means you have 3 unread notifications.

The badge updates in real-time as you receive notifications.

Click the icon to see your notifications and mark them as read.

## Managing Notification Overload

If you receive too many notifications:

**Option 1: Disable Notification Types**
Go to Notification Settings and disable types you don't need (e.g., "Team Member Activity").

**Option 2: Switch to Digest**
Change from Real-Time to Weekly digest. You get one email per week instead of constant notifications.

**Option 3: Enable Do Not Disturb**
Set work hours and disable notifications outside those hours.

**Option 4: Disable Email Notifications**
Keep in-app notifications but disable email. Check the Notifications page manually instead.

## Notification Best Practices

**Enable Real-Time for Critical Notifications**
- Project approvals (so you know when client signs off)
- Urgent team messages

**Use Digest for Low-Priority Notifications**
- Team member activity
- Content Agent updates
- System announcements

**Check Notifications Regularly**
Even if you disable email, check your in-app notifications weekly to catch important updates.

## Troubleshooting

**Q: I'm not receiving notifications.**
A: Check:
1. Notifications are enabled in Settings
2. Your notification channels are enabled (email, in-app, etc.)
3. Check your email spam folder (notifications may be marked as spam)
4. Refresh your browser (sometimes notifications are cached)
5. Contact support if notifications still don't appear

**Q: I'm receiving too many notifications.**
A: Go to Notification Settings and:
1. Disable notification types you don't need
2. Switch from Real-Time to Digest
3. Set Do Not Disturb hours
4. Disable email and rely on in-app only

**Q: A notification says someone approved my project but I don't see the project.**
A: Click the action link in the notification to go directly to the project. Or navigate to your Projects page and search for the project name.
